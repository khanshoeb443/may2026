About Us

Avanti Services is a logistics and transportation service provider offering practical cargo movement solutions for businesses. We help customers arrange reliable transportation, express consolidated cargo services across Maharashtra, full truck load (FTL) transportation, and international air freight forwarding for import and export shipments.

Our focus is to provide convenient, cost-effective and customer-oriented logistics solutions that help businesses move their goods efficiently.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide accessible and efficient logistics solutions that help businesses transport their goods with confidence.

🔭 Our Vision

To develop Avanti Services into a trusted logistics partner for businesses across Maharashtra and expand our network through strong customer relationships and reliable service partnerships.
